# Maychant A — softer inner curve

A focused refinement of the selected A / Shorter Roof logo. The two inner peaks and central dip of the M are redrawn as four smooth cubic curves, giving them a broader, rounder transition. The roof, external outline, foot ends, proportions, colors and wordmark remain as in version A.

- `maychant-a-softer-comparison.png`: previous A and the softer drawing, including close-ups.
- `vectors/maychant-a-softer-symbol.svg`: editable symbol.
- `vectors/maychant-a-softer-horizontal.svg`: editable full logo with the Cabinet Grotesk 800 wordmark.
- Additional SVGs provide the monochrome symbol and versions for dark backgrounds.
- `maychant-a-softer-symbol.png`: transparent 1024 px symbol.
- `maychant-a-softer-preview.png`: full-logo preview on white.

Colors: cobalt `#1557FF` and navy `#101E42`. The actual wordmark is outlined Cabinet Grotesk Extrabold 800. Typography credit: Cabinet Grotesk by Indian Type Foundry (ITF); ITF owns the font's trademarks and copyrights. No font file is included or required to display the outlined SVGs.

The SVGs contain editable paths, with two colors or fewer. The roof path is verified identical to the selected A, and the visible logo bounds are unchanged. The large before/after comparison was rendered and visually inspected. Validation details are in `validation.json`.
