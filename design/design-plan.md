## Image analysis before implementation

Hero reference: centered navy and cobalt two-line headline dominates; broad white margins; thin connection arcs behind three business photographs. Central image occupies ~40% of stage, side images ~25% with inward perspective. Heading tight geometric bold, body quieter, two CTAs. We reduce the comp's very long subtitle to a readable two-line 600px measure and fit primary action on a small laptop. Keep wordmark lowercase with bespoke interlocking arches.

Pathways reference: asymmetrical 2:1:1 accordion with photography on the active panel, navy and pale cyan panels. Retain open editorial heading and panel concept; remove duplicated navigation, oversized numerical decoration and extraneous owner tag. Use real buttons with aria-expanded and no hover-only information. Mobile stacks panels.

Journey reference: navy field, cyan path, physical white document and key-transfer image. Preserve strong diagonal layer relationship but keep copy as accessible HTML. Original generated keys photograph is separate from the design comp. Scroll changes document topics in three stages; explicit buttons also work. Sample content clearly labeled.

Closing reference: split heading and hairline FAQ rows followed by full-width cobalt CTA. Preserve typography, whitespace and proportions. Footer links act on real content. No invented social proof.

Generated reference photos are illustrative brand imagery, not customers or business listings. Five original assets are warm with teal details and natural lighting; use consistent grading, no outside-site asset copying.
