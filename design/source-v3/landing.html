A few things
you might be wondering.

Can I buy or list a business yet?+

Not yet. We're bringing together interested buyers and business owners before launch. The waitlist is the first step; there are no live listings or transactions on this site.

What kinds of businesses is this for?+

We're starting with independent small businesses: neighborhood shops, cafés, repair businesses, local services and the many places people rely on. Joining helps us understand where the interest is.

Do I need to be ready right now?+

No. You might be exploring ownership for the first time, or thinking about selling a few years from now. You're welcome to join. It doesn't commit you to a purchase or sale.

What happens after I join?+

We save your details and your interest in buying, selling or both. We'll email you about launch and early access as Maychant develops. There isn't a fixed launch date yet.

Maychant waitlist

Your details,
treated with care.

When you join, Maychant stores your name, email, interest in buying or selling, optional location and consent timestamp in its hosted database.

We use this information to manage the waitlist and contact you about launch and early access. Your details are not posted publicly or sold. No advertising trackers are installed. We keep short-lived hashed request identifiers to limit spam; we do not store raw IP addresses in the waitlist database.

To ask about your details, update them or leave the waitlist, email krishdw@umich.edu.

Joining does not create a business listing, guarantee a match or commit you to a transaction.
